## 四、长描述 / Long Description

### 简体中文（TapTap 商店介绍）

> **几何突围** —— 一场发生在崩塌的霓虹模拟场里的几何突围战。
>
> 你不是英雄，也不是机甲。你是一颗刚刚觉醒的 **向量三角**，被困在一个不断收缩的几何模拟空间里。四周的抽象敌潮——Chaser 追猎者、Skimmer 掠袭者、Charger 冲锋者、Splitter 分裂体、Shooter 射击体——都在向你逼近；每一波结束，场地的 Compression（压缩）、Surge（脉冲）、Overclock（超频）、Glitch（故障）四种规则都会换新。
>
> 你不会主动开枪，但你不是孤身一人。战斗中你可以校准最多六种空间模块，让它们成为你的延伸：
>
> ⚡ **追踪光束 (Trace Beam)** — 窄束自动锁定敌人的持续光刃
> 🔄 **轨道种子 (Orbit Seed)** — 围绕你旋转的攻击节点
> 💥 **脉冲绽放 (Pulse Bloom)** — 周期性向四周释放的光波冲击
> 🛡️ **护壳灯 (Shell Lantern)** — 可充能的护盾，挡在完整度之前
> 💣 **锚点地雷 (Anchor Mine)** — 自动布设的近炸地雷，敌人靠近时引爆
> 🪝 **矢量钩尾 (Vector Hook)** — 移动时在你身后留下持续造成伤害的轨迹
>
> 每次升级，三个随机校准选项摆在面前；你可以专精一路，也可以把六种模块同时堆到极限——构筑决定你能走多远。
>
> 第六波，Boss **核心破除者 (Core Breaker)** 降临。六边核心、磁红不稳定核心、紫色能量裂纹。它不只是攻击，还会周期性召唤随从，必须用光束、地雷、脉冲的范围伤害同时压制——这是一场对 DPS 与构筑的终极考验。
>
> **几何突围** —— 没有肉鸽的疲惫，只有突围的瞬间快感。
>
> 🟦 Neon Vector Geometry 美术风格 · 深蓝海军色 / 青色向量 / 橙金 Boss / 磁红敌潮
> 🟦 双语 UI（中英切换） · 手机虚拟摇杆 + 电脑键鼠双端支持
> 🟦 短局 + 高重玩价值 · 一局 5–15 分钟 · 适合碎片时间
>
> **当前为 Demo Build** —— 包含 8 波战斗、1 个 Boss、6 种模块、随机构筑校准、完整断点重启与遥测；后续将开放 Workshop 外观工坊与更多章节。

### English（TapTap Store Description）

> **Geometry Breakout** — break out from a collapsing neon geometry simulation.
>
> You are not a hero. You are not a mech. You are a newly awakened **Vector Triangle**, trapped inside an abstract arena that is constantly contracting and reshaping itself. Hostile geometry is closing in from every direction — Chasers, Skimmers, Chargers, Splitters, Shooters — each with a distinct movement, telegraph, and pressure pattern. Between waves, the arena cycles between four field rules: Compression, Surge, Overclock, and Glitch.
>
> You don't aim, but you're not alone. Calibrate up to six spatial modules and let them fight for you:
>
> ⚡ **Trace Beam** — a narrow auto-tracking beam that locks onto targets
> 🔄 **Orbit Seed** — a rotating strike node circling your chassis
> 💥 **Pulse Bloom** — periodic radial bursts that push enemies back
> 🛡️ **Shell Lantern** — a rechargeable shield that absorbs hits before your Integrity
> 💣 **Anchor Mine** — proximity mines that arm, then detonate on contact
> 🪝 **Vector Hook** — a damaging movement trail that fades as you advance
>
> Every level-up, three random calibrations are presented. Specialize one path, or stack all six modules — your build decides how far you go.
>
> On Wave 8, the **Core Breaker** appears: an orange-gold hex-core boss with a magenta unstable core, violet energy cracks, and a four-stage DPS check. Mines, pulse bursts, and beams all matter. This is the final breakout.
>
> **Geometry Breakout** — no roguelite fatigue, only the breakout moment.
>
> 🟦 Neon Vector Geometry art direction — deep navy arena / electric cyan player / orange-gold boss / magenta hostile pressure
> 🟦 Bilingual UI (English + Simplified Chinese) with runtime switching
> 🟦 Touch joystick on mobile · WASD/arrows on PC · 5–15 min runs · perfect for short sessions
>
> **Currently Demo Build** — 8 waves, 1 boss, 6 modules, random calibration picks, full restart loop, and run telemetry. Workshop cosmetics and additional chapters coming soon.
